🔥 Ab Crunch Machine
Ab Crunch Machine
Primary: Rectus Abdominis · Secondary: Obliques
How to perform
  • 1Adjust the seat and foot roller to fit your height.
  • 2Cross arms over chest or grip handles behind your head.
  • 3Crunch forward by contracting abs — not by pulling with your neck.
  • 4Hold the peak squeeze 1 second, then slowly return to start.
Recommended 3 Sets × 15–20 Reps
🌀 Rotary Torso Machine
Rotary Torso Machine
Primary: Obliques · Secondary: Transverse Abdominis
How to perform
  • 1Sit and position the chest pad against your upper body.
  • 2Cross arms over the pad. Keep hips and legs completely still.
  • 3Rotate your torso to one side through the full range of motion.
  • 4Return to center and rotate to the other side. Alternate evenly.
Recommended 3 Sets × 12 Reps each side
⚡ Cable Crunch
Cable Crunch
Primary: Rectus Abdominis · Secondary: Hip Flexors
How to perform
  • 1Attach rope to high pulley. Kneel facing the machine.
  • 2Hold rope beside your head. Hips stay fixed — don't sit back.
  • 3Crunch down, bringing elbows toward knees. Round your spine.
  • 4Hold the contraction, then rise slowly back to the start position.
Recommended 3 Sets × 15–20 Reps
🏔️ Roman Chair / Back Extension
Roman Chair / Back Extension
Primary: Erector Spinae · Secondary: Glutes, Hamstrings
How to perform
  • 1Position yourself face down, hip bones on the pad, feet hooked.
  • 2Cross arms over your chest or hold a plate for added resistance.
  • 3Lower your torso until almost 90° to the floor. Feel the stretch.
  • 4Raise back to a straight body position. Do not hyperextend.
Recommended 3 Sets × 12–15 Reps
