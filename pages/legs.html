🦵 Leg Press Machine
Leg Press Machine
Primary: Quads, Glutes · Secondary: Hamstrings, Calves
How to perform
  • 1Sit in the machine, place feet shoulder-width apart on the platform.
  • 2Release the safety handles and lower the platform by bending knees to 90°.
  • 3Press through your heels to extend legs — don't lock knees at the top.
  • 4Return slowly. Keep your lower back pressed against the seat.
Recommended 4 Sets × 10–12 Reps
📐 Leg Extension Machine
Leg Extension Machine
Primary: Quadriceps (all 4 heads)
How to perform
  • 1Adjust the pad to rest just above your ankle. Sit upright.
  • 2Grip the side handles for stability.
  • 3Lift the pad by extending your knees until legs are straight.
  • 4Hold 1 second at the top, then lower with control. Don't swing.
Recommended 3 Sets × 12–15 Reps
🔄 Lying Leg Curl
Lying Leg Curl Machine
Primary: Hamstrings · Secondary: Calves, Glutes
How to perform
  • 1Lie face down, position the roller pad just below your calves.
  • 2Grip the handles and keep your hips pressed down.
  • 3Curl legs upward as far as possible. Squeeze hamstrings at the top.
  • 4Lower slowly — 3 full seconds on the negative.
Recommended 3 Sets × 10–12 Reps
⬆️ Standing Calf Raise
Standing Calf Raise Machine
Primary: Gastrocnemius · Secondary: Soleus
How to perform
  • 1Step onto the platform, shoulders under the pads. Feet hip-width.
  • 2Lower your heels as far as comfortable — feel the calf stretch.
  • 3Rise up onto your toes as high as possible. Pause at the top.
  • 4Descend slowly. Full range of motion on every rep.
Recommended 4 Sets × 15–20 Reps
