💪 Bicep Curl Machine
Bicep Curl Machine
Primary: Biceps Brachii · Secondary: Brachialis
How to perform
  • 1Adjust seat so elbows rest on the pad at the bottom of the range.
  • 2Grip handles with palms facing up (supinated).
  • 3Curl the handles up toward your shoulders. Squeeze at the top.
  • 4Lower slowly — take 2–3 seconds on the way down.
Recommended 3 Sets × 10–12 Reps
⬇️ Tricep Pushdown
Tricep Cable Pushdown
Primary: Triceps Brachii (all 3 heads)
How to perform
  • 1Attach rope or straight bar to the high pulley.
  • 2Stand close, elbows pinned to your sides at 90°.
  • 3Push down until arms are fully extended. Flare rope ends outward.
  • 4Return to 90° slowly. Keep elbows fixed throughout.
Recommended 3 Sets × 12–15 Reps
🔁 Preacher Curl Machine
Preacher Curl Machine
Primary: Biceps Brachii (long head) · Secondary: Brachialis
How to perform
  • 1Adjust seat so armpits rest at the top of the angled pad.
  • 2Grip handles with palms up. Arms hang nearly straight.
  • 3Curl up fully, hold 1 second at the peak contraction.
  • 4Lower with full control — don't drop the weight.
Recommended 3 Sets × 10–12 Reps
🦾 Overhead Tricep Extension
Overhead Tricep Extension
Primary: Triceps Brachii (long head)
How to perform
  • 1Set cable pulley to the lowest position with a rope attachment.
  • 2Face away from the machine, grip rope overhead with both hands.
  • 3Extend arms forward until fully straight. Don't flare elbows.
  • 4Bend elbows to return. Feel the stretch in the tricep.
Recommended 3 Sets × 12–15 Reps
