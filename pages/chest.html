🏋️ Chest Press Machine
Chest Press Machine
Primary: Pectoralis Major · Secondary: Triceps, Front Delts
How to perform
  • 1Adjust the seat so handles are at chest height.
  • 2Sit back firmly, feet flat on the floor.
  • 3Grip handles, elbows at 90°. Push forward until arms are nearly straight.
  • 4Slowly return to start. Keep tension — don't let the weight stack touch.
Recommended 3 Sets × 10–12 Reps
🔄 Cable Fly Machine
Cable Fly Machine
Primary: Pectoralis Major (inner) · Secondary: Front Delts
How to perform
  • 1Set both pulleys to shoulder height. Stand in the center.
  • 2Grab handles, step forward with one foot for balance.
  • 3With a slight elbow bend, bring hands together in front of chest.
  • 4Squeeze at the center, then open arms slowly back to start.
Recommended 3 Sets × 12–15 Reps
🦅 Pec Deck Machine
Pec Deck (Butterfly)
Primary: Pectoralis Major · Secondary: Front Delts
How to perform
  • 1Adjust seat so arms are parallel to the floor when gripping pads.
  • 2Sit upright, press back into the seat pad.
  • 3Bring forearms together in a hugging motion. Squeeze the chest.
  • 4Return slowly — don't allow the weight to yank your arms back.
Recommended 3 Sets × 12–15 Reps
📐 Incline Smith Machine
Incline Smith Machine Press
Primary: Upper Pectoralis · Secondary: Triceps, Front Delts
How to perform
  • 1Set bench to 30–45° incline. Position bar above upper chest.
  • 2Lie back, grip bar slightly wider than shoulder width.
  • 3Unrack and lower bar slowly to upper chest.
  • 4Press up powerfully. Re-rack by rotating wrists at the top.
Recommended 4 Sets × 8–10 Reps
